COMPANY: Infosys Limited (NSE: INFY)
QUARTER: Q3 FY2025
DATE: January 16, 2025
TYPE: Earnings Call Transcript

MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY - CEO Salil Parekh:
We are pleased to report revenue of $4.7 billion for Q3 FY2025, 
representing 7.1% growth in constant currency terms. Our large deal 
wins totaled $2.5 billion, reflecting continued client confidence 
in our AI-first strategy. We are seeing strong demand in financial 
services and manufacturing verticals.

MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY - CFO Jayesh Sanghrajka:
Operating margins came in at 21.3%, expanding 50 basis points 
sequentially. This improvement was driven by better utilization rates 
at 82.5% and reduced subcontracting costs. We expect margins to 
remain in the 20-22% band for Q4 FY2025. Employee costs remain 
our largest expense at 58% of revenue.

ANALYST Q&A:
Q (JPMorgan): Can you elaborate on the pricing environment?
A (CEO): We are seeing stable pricing across most segments. However, 
discretionary spending remains muted in the BFSI sector. We expect 
recovery in H2 FY2026 as interest rate uncertainty resolves.

Q (Goldman Sachs): What is your headcount guidance?
A (CFO): We plan to hire 15,000 freshers in FY2025. Attrition has 
stabilized at 12.9%, the lowest in 10 quarters. We are investing 
heavily in reskilling employees on GenAI and cloud platforms.

CORPORATE ACTIONS:
Interim dividend of Rs 21 per share declared.
Buyback program of Rs 9,200 crore approved by board.
