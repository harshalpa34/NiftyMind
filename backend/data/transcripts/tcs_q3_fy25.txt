COMPANY: Tata Consultancy Services (NSE: TCS)
QUARTER: Q3 FY2025
DATE: January 9, 2025
TYPE: Earnings Call Transcript

MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY - CEO K Krithivasan:
TCS reported revenue of $7.5 billion for Q3 FY2025, growing 5.6% 
in constant currency. We continue to lead in AI transformation 
engagements, with over 600 AI projects delivered this quarter. 
Our order book remains healthy with total contract value of $10.2 billion.

MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY - CFO Samir Seksaria:
EBIT margins stood at 24.5%, within our guided range of 26-28% 
after accounting for wage revision impact. Net income grew 12% 
year-over-year to Rs 12,380 crore. Free cash flow conversion 
remained strong at 98.5% of net income.

ANALYST Q&A:
Q (Morgan Stanley): How is GenAI adoption progressing with clients?
A (CEO): Clients are moving from proof-of-concept to production 
deployments. We see GenAI adding 1-2% to revenue growth in FY2026. 
Our proprietary platform WisdomNext is gaining strong traction in 
the BFSI and healthcare sectors.

Q (UBS): Comment on North America demand environment.
A (CEO): North America grew 3.2% and remains our largest market at 
53% of revenue. BFSI clients are cautious on discretionary spend 
but are prioritizing cost optimization and compliance projects.

CORPORATE ACTIONS:
Final dividend of Rs 76 per share recommended.
Special dividend of Rs 400 per share announced.
Share buyback of Rs 17,000 crore at Rs 4,500 per share approved.
