COMPANY: HDFC Bank Limited (NSE: HDFCBANK)
QUARTER: Q3 FY2025
DATE: January 22, 2025
TYPE: Earnings Call Transcript

MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY - CEO Sashidhar Jagdishan:
Net interest income grew 8.1% year-over-year to Rs 30,650 crore. 
Net interest margin compressed to 3.4% from 3.6% a year ago, 
reflecting higher cost of deposits in a rising rate environment. 
Our loan book grew 12% with retail loans comprising 54% of total advances.

MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY - CFO Srinivasan Vaidyanathan:
Asset quality remained stable with gross NPA at 1.26%. Provisions 
were Rs 4,217 crore, reflecting proactive credit risk management. 
Capital adequacy ratio stands at 19.3%, providing strong buffer 
for growth. Return on equity was 15.8% for the quarter.

ANALYST Q&A:
Q (Jefferies): What is your deposit growth strategy?
A (CEO): We are focusing on granular retail deposits through our 
branch network of 8,300+ branches. CD ratio has improved to 87% 
from 90% a year ago. We expect NIMs to stabilize at 3.4-3.5% 
as deposit competition moderates.

Q (Nomura): Commentary on credit costs going forward.
A (CFO): We expect credit costs to remain in the 50-60 bps range. 
Unsecured retail stress is manageable and within our risk appetite. 
MSME book continues to perform well with NPA below industry average.

CORPORATE ACTIONS:
Dividend of Rs 19.50 per share declared.
Rights issue consideration deferred to next quarter.
